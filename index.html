About

Hello · E kú àbọ̀ · Sannu da zuwa · Sawubona · Hola

I am a lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at the University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg. My work sits at the meeting point of numerical analysis and the physical sciences: I design and analyse numerical methods, and I use them to study fluid flow, transport phenomena and other dynamical systems.

Before joining Wits I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rhodes University, where I worked on multidomain pseudospectral methods for fractional differential equations. I hold a PhD in Mathematics from Nelson Mandela University, and an MSc and BSc (Hons) from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria.
