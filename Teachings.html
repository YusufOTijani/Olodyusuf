Teaching Philosophy

"I firmly believe that students come from diverse educational backgrounds, possess unique characteristics, and belong to varied demographics and social statuses. As a result, it is crucial to create an inclusive learning environment that actively avoids exclusion and segregation. Such an environment should prioritize equity, ensuring that every student has access to the resources, opportunities, and support they need to thrive, regardless of their individual circumstances."
