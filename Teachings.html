Teaching & mentorship

Fostering an inclusive, equitable and rigorous environment for the next generation of mathematicians.

Teaching philosophy

Students come from diverse educational backgrounds, possess unique characteristics, and belong to varied demographics and social statuses. It is therefore crucial to create an inclusive learning environment that actively avoids exclusion and segregation — one that prioritises equity, ensuring every student has access to the resources, opportunities and support they need to thrive, regardless of their individual circumstances.
